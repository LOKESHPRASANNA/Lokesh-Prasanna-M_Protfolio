My Approach

Design Thinking Process

A structured, human-centered framework I follow to create solutions that truly resonate with users.

01
👥

Understand Users

Empathize with target users through observation, interviews, and immersion in their environment.

02
🔬

User Research

Gather qualitative & quantitative data via surveys, usability tests, and competitive analysis.

03
🎯

Problem Definition

Synthesize research into a clear problem statement using affinity maps and user personas.

04
✏️

Wireframing

Sketch low-fidelity layouts to explore structure, navigation, and content hierarchy rapidly.

05
🎨

High Fidelity UI

Create pixel-perfect screens in Figma with full design systems, colors, and typography.

06
⚡

Prototype

Build interactive prototypes with realistic micro-interactions and animated transitions.

07
🧪

User Testing

Validate designs through usability sessions, A/B tests, and iterative feedback loops.

08
🚀

Final Design

Deliver developer-ready assets, design specs, and handoff documentation in Figma.
